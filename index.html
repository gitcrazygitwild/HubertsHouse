How the three predictions work

Huckleberry’s SweetSpot is its last stored prediction; opening Huckleberry may be needed to calculate a new one. Hubert’s Purrfect Window is an independent daytime estimate using up to 14 days of logged sleep. It prefers wake windows around the same time of day, uses the median of up to 20 recent windows, and shows the middle half of those windows with at least 10 minutes either side. It needs at least three windows across two days. Mia’s Catnap Forecast works differently: it compares only the same nap of the day (wake-up → nap 1, nap 1 → nap 2…), counts recent days more (each day’s weight halves every three days), adds a small capped growth trend, and nudges the window when the last nap was shorter or longer than usual — by how much is learned from Frankie’s own history. It needs that nap on at least three days. Every guess is saved at wake-up and scored on the Frankie page. Missing logs can skew it. Follow your baby’s cues; this is a pattern estimate, not medical advice.
